### September 9, 2026 — Unicode-Normalized Local Installed-App Search — Development

Draft PR #90 (`fix/unicode-local-app-search`) was stacked directly on exact-green PR #89 head `997f34e3d8b566a9141caf00f2a58b0a860821a0` and validated at exact head `8f21fd9fea399f7608e3e2b8bca7b8c7f8e75ea7`.
