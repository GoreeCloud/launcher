### August 28, 2026 — Deterministic Multi-Page Workspace Mutations Merged

Change type or category: native Android workspace persistence; multi-page HOME ordering; Room authority validation.

GoreeCloud Launcher continued its native multi-page workspace work by adding application-owned page and item mutation contracts to WorkspacePagedPlacement. Page moves validate existing workspace state, reorder by requested position, and normalize page ranks to a contiguous sequence. Cross-page item moves preserve item identity and validate the resulting whole workspace for per-page bounds/collisions and global item uniqueness, while invalid workspace state, missing pages/items, out-of-range positions, and identity substitution fail closed. Focused JVM tests cover successful and rejected mutations.
